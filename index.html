How to Install

Inject into any sideloaded iOS app — no jailbreak needed

Download Latest .dylib GitHub Repo
STEP 1 · DOWNLOAD
Get the Dylib

Download LocationSpoofer.dylib from GitHub Releases directly to your iPhone or computer.

STEP 2 · INJECT
LiveContainer or Sideloadly

In LiveContainer, place the dylib in your Tweaks folder and turn it on. In Sideloadly, inject via Advanced Options.

STEP 3 · OPEN MENU
3-Finger Hold

Hold three fingers anywhere on screen for 0.8s to open the menu. Choose your location and tap Save Settings.
